# Excel-Sales-Analytics

Sales Report
Project Objectives:
1. Create a detailed customer performance report to evaluate individual customer contributions to revenue and profitability.(https://github.com/Amisha628/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/MY%20INDIAN%20SALES.pdf)
2. Conduct a robust comparison of market performance and sales targets to identify deviations and areas for improvement.

Purpose of Sales Analytics:
Enable businesses to evaluate their sales strategies, measure success, and make data-driven decisions that optimize performance and profitability.

Significance of Analyzing Sales Data:

Uncover sales trends to anticipate future demand and adapt strategies.
Track key performance indicators (KPIs) like revenue, customer acquisition costs, and sales cycle length.
Identify high-value customers and target segments to improve marketing and sales efficiency.
Role of Reports:

Inform customer discount policies to enhance retention and boost sales.
Equip sales teams with data to negotiate effectively and foster better customer relationships.
Highlight expansion opportunities by analyzing high-potential markets or regions.
Finance Report
Project Objectives:
1. Generate Profit and Loss (P&L) statements by fiscal year and month to ensure consistent financial monitoring.(https://github.com/Amisha628/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/Sales%20Market%20Report.pdf)(https://github.com/Amisha628/Excel-Sales-Analytics/blob/main/p_and_l_fine_tuning.pdf)
2. Create segmented P&L reports by market to evaluate regional performance and profitability.

Purpose of Financial Analytics:
Provide a comprehensive overview of financial health, support strategic decision-making, and facilitate transparent communication with stakeholders.

Significance of Analyzing Financial Data:

Benchmark financial results against industry standards and historical data.
Build a solid foundation for budgeting, forecasting, and resource allocation.
Identify trends in revenue, expenses, and profitability for informed decision-making.
Role of Reports:

Align financial planning with long-term organizational objectives.
Foster confidence in stakeholders by demonstrating financial stability and strategic foresight.
Drive data-informed discussions to prioritize investments and manage risks.
Technical & Soft Skills
Technical Skills:

 Expertise in ETL processes (Extract, Transform, Load) for efficient data handling.
 Advanced knowledge of Power Query for creating and managing date tables.
 Ability to derive fiscal months, quarters, and other time-based metrics.
 Proficiency in building and managing data model relationships using Power Pivot.
 Integration of supplementary datasets into existing models for enhanced analysis.
 Mastery of DAX for creating calculated columns and measures to derive insights.
Soft Skills:

 Strong understanding of sales and finance report structures and their implications.
 A user-focused approach to designing accessible and meaningful reports.
 Skilled in optimizing workflows for efficient and accurate report generation.
 Systematic planning and execution of report development, ensuring clarity and precision.
This structured approach ensures clarity in objectives, relevance of analytics, and the necessary skills to drive impactful results in both sales and finance domains.
